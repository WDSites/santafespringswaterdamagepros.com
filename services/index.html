Choosing the right service

Water damage is not one job. It is a chain of jobs, and which link you need depends on where you are in the chain. Standing water needs removal first. Wet but not flooded structures need drying. Ruined materials need cleanup. Dry but damaged rooms need repair. Mold needs its own containment and clearance steps.

Each page below explains one service in detail, including what equipment is used, what affects the cost and what to do while you wait for the crew.

How the services fit together

Most losses use several services in sequence. Emergency water removal pulls standing water out. Water mitigation dries the structure and stops secondary damage. Water damage cleanup removes ruined materials and sanitizes. Water damage repair rebuilds.

Flood damage restoration applies when the water came from outside or from a sewer, which changes the safety rules. Mold remediation applies when moisture has gone unchecked long enough for mold to grow.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the situation on the phone. If water is still on the floor, start with removal and mitigation. If everything is dry but damaged, you are in repair. If you see or smell mold, ask about remediation and the leak that caused it. The dispatcher will sort this out with you.
