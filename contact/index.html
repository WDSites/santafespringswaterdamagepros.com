Call the 24/7 emergency line

Phone is the fastest way to reach us. The line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends and holidays. This site has no contact form, because a form cannot answer a question about a wet ceiling at midnight.

You can also email contact@santafespringswaterdamagepros.com for non-urgent questions. For active water, sewage or mold you want checked, call.

Business address and hours

Santa Fe Springs Water Damage Pros, 13608 Imperial Hwy, Santa Fe Springs, CA 90670. Phone (562) 271-5850. Emergency line hours: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help the call go faster. You do not need all of them, and the dispatcher will ask for anything missing.

  • ✓
    Your address and the nearest cross street
  • ✓
    Where the water is coming from and whether it has been shut off
  • ✓
    How long the area has been wet
  • ✓
    Whether the water looks clean, gray or like sewage
  • ✓
    Which rooms and materials are wet, such as carpet, hardwood, drywall or ceilings
  • ✓
    Whether water has reached outlets or the electrical panel
  • ✓
    Your insurance company name, if you plan to file a claim

If water has reached the panel or outlets, or you smell gas, leave the area and call the utility or 911 first, then call us.
