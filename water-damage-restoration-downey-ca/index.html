Downey's water system and what your leak has to do with it

The City of Downey Utilities Division runs water, sewer and storm drain systems, with 20 groundwater wells, 276 miles of water mains and 200 miles of sewer main. The city does not use imported water directly and keeps 3 Metropolitan Water District connections for emergencies. That means your tap supply is mostly local groundwater.

Parts of Downey east of the San Gabriel River, south of I-5 and north of Cecilia Avenue are served by other purveyors, so check your bill if your address sits near those edges.

What to do and what not to do first in Downey

Safety comes before drying. Do not walk through standing water if the lights, outlets or panel could be wet, and do not run a household vacuum on water. Published industry guidance, the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, sorts water by contamination level, so a gray or sewage-smelling flood is handled differently from a clean supply-line leak.

Shut off the main valve if you can reach it, move valuables to a dry room, and photograph the damage before anything is thrown out. Then call the emergency line and describe what is wet.

If water is still spreading

Shut the main valve

Turn it clockwise at the house. Open a low faucet to drain the pipes and ease pressure on the break.

Mind the electrics

Cut breakers only if the panel is dry. If the ceiling is dripping through a light fixture, stay out of the room and phone the utility first.

Move valuables

Set boxes, electronics and bedding on beds, counters or tables. Rolled rugs go onto dry floor, not wet carpet.

Photograph everything

Capture the source, the water line on walls and damaged contents. Time-stamp photos help any claim.

Downey rain, flood maps and the river boundaries

The city's hazard plan lists a FIRM date of 09/26/08 and describes Downey as a non-special flood hazard area, with average annual rain of about 17 inches concentrated in winter. The wettest recent month was February 2020 at 3.02 inches. Even in a low-risk area, a clogged gutter or failed sump can flood a garage, so clear drains before winter.

Rio Hondo is the west boundary and the San Gabriel River the east boundary. The plan includes Whittier Narrows Dam failure scenarios, so look up your address at the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

What Downey's housing numbers mean for claims

Downey has about 35,543 housing units and 34,876 households, with a 51.1 percent owner-occupied rate. Roughly half of households rent, so landlord notice and tenant renters insurance both matter. If you rent, report the leak in writing and keep your photos. Renters insurance may cover your belongings even when the building is the landlord's responsibility.

Permits for repairs in Downey

The Building and Safety Division, on the first floor of City Hall at 11111 Brookshire Avenue, says new construction, additions, alterations or repairs to an existing building generally need a permit. Its FAQ also lists re-pipes, water heaters, electrical, mechanical, stucco and window or door changeouts. Ask the division what your project needs before rebuilding after a loss.
