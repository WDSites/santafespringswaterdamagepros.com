If water is still spreading

Water damage restoration La Mirada begins with these steps while crews are on the way.

Close the main valve

Clockwise at the house. If it will not turn, shut the supply at the meter if you can, or phone your provider.

Protect the panel

Only cut breakers to wet rooms if the panel is dry. Water at outlets or fixtures means stay out and call the utility or 911.

Raise the valuables

Get shoes, electronics, papers and clothes off the floor. Prop furniture on foil or plastic blocks.

Take photos

Record the source and each wet area before cleanup. Include the make and age of a failed appliance.

Before the crew gets to your La Mirada home

A few safety steps matter more than any cleanup. Do not touch electrical equipment while standing in water, do not use heaters or fans that have been wet, and keep children and pets out of the affected rooms. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Document what you can see with wide and close photos, write down when the water started, and set aside wet items instead of discarding them. Those records help whoever reviews your insurance claim.

Mid-1950s homes and aging plumbing in La Mirada

The Neff family sold 2,218 acres to Jack Spears in 1953, and Louis Halper took control in 1954. About 7,800 homes were built by 1956. Homes from the mid-1950s are about 70 years old now, so old supply lines, shutoff valves and water heaters deserve a look.

Check the shutoff valves under sinks and behind toilets for corrosion, and replace braided hoses at washers. A valve that no longer turns is useless in an emergency.

Who supplies water in La Mirada

No single utility serves the whole city. Golden State Water's Central Basin East system serves portions of La Mirada, and Suburban Water Systems owns the La Mirada Water System, which draws on the Main San Gabriel and Central Basins plus purchased imported water. Check your bill to see which company serves you.

Sewer connections run through the Los Angeles County Sanitation Districts. If a backup involves the lateral under the street, your provider or the districts may need to be involved.

Permits through the county in La Mirada

La Mirada's Building and Safety Division is run by Los Angeles County under contract with the city, with a counter at City Hall, 13700 La Mirada Boulevard. The city says a residential building permit is required to build, modify, remodel, repair or demolish any building or structure, and plumbing and electrical permits cover alterations to those systems. Ask the counter before starting any water-related repair.

Parks, Creek Park and flood questions in La Mirada

Creek Park, at 12021 Santa Gertrudes Avenue, is a 26-acre passive park along a flood control channel. That channel is a reminder that stormwater is part of the city's design. If your home is near it, look up your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask your insurer about flood coverage, since standard homeowners policies exclude flood.

Households and insurance in La Mirada

La Mirada has about 15,701 housing units, 15,304 households and a 76.1 percent owner-occupied rate. High ownership means most losses are claims the owner files themselves. Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered while gradual leaks often are not, so photograph the cause, keep receipts and ask your insurer before discarding materials.
